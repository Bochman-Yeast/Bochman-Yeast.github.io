Join us

Interested in joining the lab?

We're a collaborative, hands-on group working across biochemistry, genetics, and fermentation microbiology. This page is a starting point — reach out and we'll talk specifics.

Graduate students

Prospective PhD students should apply through one of IU's graduate programs that the lab is affiliated with — the Biochemistry Graduate Program or the Genome, Cell & Developmental Biology (GCDB) Program — and can mention interest in rotating through the lab in their application or by reaching out directly.

Postdoctoral researchers

We're generally open to strong postdoctoral candidates whose interests align with our work on DNA helicases, genome stability, telomere maintenance, or fermentation microbiology. Email with your CV and a short note about your research interests.

Undergraduate researchers

IU Bloomington undergraduates interested in research experience are welcome to reach out. Include your year, major, relevant coursework or lab experience, and what you're hoping to get out of the experience.

What we look for

Curiosity, persistence, and a willingness to get your hands dirty — literally, in the case of the fermentation side of the lab. No specific technique is a prerequisite; we'll teach what's needed.
