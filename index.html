Studying genome integrity through the lens of DNA helicases

The Bochman Lab investigates DNA helicases: the motor proteins that unwind DNA to enable replication, repair, and telomere maintenance. We work across genome stability, PIF1- and RecQ4-family helicases, telomerase regulation, and — because good science and good fermentation share a lot of biochemistry — wild yeast and brewing.

Four questions we keep coming back to

Each project below sits inside the same big question: how do cells preserve genomic information, and what goes wrong when they can't?

RecQ4 helicases & genome stability

How the RecQ4-family helicase Hrq1 and its human relative RECQL4 protect the genome, and what happens when they fail.

Telomerase regulation by DNA helicases

How helicases like Pif1 and Hrq1 modulate telomerase activity and telomere-binding proteins such as Cdc13.

Post-translational modification of Pif1

How acetylation and other modifications tune Pif1 helicase activity at G-quadruplexes and beyond.

Fermentation science

Wild yeast bioprospecting, brewing microbiology, and community-embedded science education.
